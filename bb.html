Peer Review Criteria

When reviewing another guild's artefact, provide structured feedback on:

1
Strengths
What did the guild do well? Be specific — reference their content.
2
Areas for Improvement
What could be developed further? Suggest how, not just what.
3
Rating (1–5)
1 = Needs significant development  ·  3 = Meets expectations  ·  5 = Exceptional
4
One Key Takeaway
What is the single most useful thing this guild's work taught your team?
